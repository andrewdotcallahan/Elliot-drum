BabyBand Jam — Support

BabyBand Jam is a music toy for toddlers: drums, guitar, xylophone, trombone, piano, and bongos, with nothing to buy, no ads, and no internet required.

Tips

  • Switch instruments: press and hold the ♪ button in the top-right corner for about a second and a half.
  • Toddler-proof the device: use iOS Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the side button in the app) so little fingers can't leave the app.
  • Xylophone songs: tap ⭐ Twinkle or ⭐ Mary and follow the glowing bar.
