BabyBand Jam — Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

The short version

BabyBand Jam collects no data. None.

The long version

BabyBand Jam is a music toy for toddlers. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data of any kind. Specifically, the app has:

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No accounts, sign-ins, or user profiles
  • No network access — the app works entirely offline
  • No access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos
  • No in-app purchases

The only thing the app stores is which instrument was last selected, saved locally on the device and never transmitted anywhere.

Children's privacy

BabyBand Jam is designed for very young children. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, no information about children (or anyone else) is ever gathered or processed.
